# Benchmark publication policy

Public: development cases, retired holdout, public lifecycle cases, schemas, scorers and upstream benchmark registry. The active mock/lifecycle holdout remains under internal e2e/model/sealed. Public reports disclose aggregate scores and SHA-256 commitments, never active prompts or answers. Rotate after material training changes. Do not train on benchmarks/. Official questions are fetched from official harnesses and pinned at run time; they are not copied here.
